# Lifecycle

Status: **draft, non-normative.** Nothing here is part of a tagged policy release. Adoption records do not reference this directory.

This directory covers the layer above architecture and coding standards: how work moves from business concept to CI and back to business summaries. The stages are business concept, business process, combining new processes with existing ones, requirements engineering, architecture, engineering, coding, unit testing, user testing, CI, business-process testing, business summaries of process merging, and product-capability summaries.

The existing standards (`standards/`) are the architecture and engineering layers of this lifecycle. A lifecycle stage becomes normative only through the hub's usual process: owner approval, a source-authority decision, an enforcement mapping, and a defined adoption process ([AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)).
