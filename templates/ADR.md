# ADR-NNNN: Decision Title

## Status and date

Proposed / Accepted / Superseded; decision date; owner.

## Context

Scope, exact versions, problem, constraints, and relevant authority.

## Decision

State the accepted choice and its scope.

## Alternatives rejected and why

Record credible alternatives and concrete rejection rationale.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, compatibility, risks, enforcement, and migration/adoption effects.

## Evidence and approval

Immutable references, tests or review evidence, and explicit approval.

## Supersession

Accepted records are not silently rewritten; a later decision supersedes them explicitly.
