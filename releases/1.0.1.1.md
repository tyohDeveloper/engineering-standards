# Standards Release 1.0.1.1

Status: released 2026-10-30; tag `standards-1.0.1.1` points at the release-note finalization commit on `main`. Fourth-component change: copy-only correction, no change to rules, profiles, classes, templates or authority.

## Meaning

The status line of `standards/profiles.md` still said that the companion wiki pages the v2.2 baseline §17 delegated to were "not yet exported" and that reconciliation was "pending". Both statements were false after release 1.0.0.0: the ten pages were exported verbatim to `references/source-snapshots/programming-wiki/` and every row of `governance/wiki-reconciliation.md` is closed. The sentence now states that the pages were exported and reconciled.

## Scope

`standards/profiles.md` (status line only), `CHANGELOG.md`, `governance/versioning.md`, this note.

## Verification

`npm run validate` and `npm test` pass. No adoption record changes.

## Adoption effects
